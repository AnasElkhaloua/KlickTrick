# Klick Trick — Project Overview
Status: Approved planning baseline (October 2026)

## Product
Klick Trick is a newly established, legally registered, full-service digital agency based in Morocco. Target audiences: startups, e-commerce businesses, professionals, and established companies. Positioning: premium/high-end creative-tech, covering strategy, creativity, technology, and performance.

## Primary outcomes
- Generate qualified inquiries through a simple Contact page form.
- Offer a persistent floating WhatsApp contact option.
- Clearly present six service pillars without exaggerating experience.
- Build a fast, accessible, SEO-ready bilingual website.

## Confirmed constraints
- French is default; English is secondary. Root `/` redirects to `/fr`.
- Preserve WEBYTE Figma black, blue and mint aesthetic.
- No public pricing, dedicated quote form, or popup in V1.
- Every 'free quote' CTA goes to the localized Contact page and form.
- No fabricated client logos, case studies, testimonials, awards, performance numbers, or experience.
- Work/Case Studies and Blog should have a clear future place in architecture, without invented entries.
- Implement with Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS; deploy to Vercel.
- No CMS initially. Use content/configuration files.
- No project implementation until user approves the specification.

## Success criteria
- Functional French and English navigation and contact forms.
- Every CTA leads to a working destination.
- Mobile layout intentionally designed, not squeezed desktop.
- Clear privacy, form validation, error and success states.
- No unverified proof claims or dummy content presented as real.
