# SEO & Localization

## Target
Businesses across Morocco; French default, English secondary. Avoid unsupported claims about rankings.

## Technical
- Semantic headings, descriptive title/meta descriptions, Open Graph and Twitter metadata per locale.
- Canonicals and reciprocal FR/EN `hreflang`, with `x-default` where appropriate.
- XML sitemap with published routes only; robots configuration.
- Organization schema using verified business data only; Service schema if appropriate and factual.
- Clear internal linking among Home, Services, About, Contact.
- Fast Core Web Vitals, optimized imagery, font loading, responsive layout.
- Meaningful alt text and crawlable body content.
- Avoid publishing thin/empty blog or portfolio routes merely for SEO.

## Content
Natural French copy for Morocco, supported by equivalent English content. Service pages should answer what is offered, who it is for, and how to inquire. Do not keyword stuff or fabricate testimonials.

## Analytics
Prepare privacy-aware analytics/events for quote CTA clicks, WhatsApp clicks, and successful contact submissions; only activate tools with proper configuration and disclosures.
