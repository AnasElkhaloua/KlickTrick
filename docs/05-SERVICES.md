# Services — Six Pillars

Each pillar should have a concise, benefit-oriented summary, genuine deliverables, and a CTA to the localized Contact page. Do not promise guaranteed results.

1. **Digital Marketing:** SEO and local SEO, social media marketing, Google Ads/PPC, content marketing, email marketing, lead generation.
2. **Branding & Creative:** brand strategy, logo design, visual identity, brand guidelines, graphic design, creative campaigns, photography/video production.
3. **Web & Technology:** website design/development, e-commerce, landing pages, UI/UX, web/app development, WordPress, maintenance, conversion rate optimization.
4. **Social Media:** strategy, account management, content creation, reels/short-form video, community management, ads, analytics/reporting.
5. **AI & Automation:** AI chatbots, AI customer support, marketing automation, CRM automation, workflow automation, AI content systems, AI integrations.
6. **Strategy & Analytics:** digital strategy, market research, competitor analysis, analytics/tracking, conversion optimization, marketing funnels, performance reporting.

## UX
- Homepage: six concise cards, not full feature catalogs.
- Services page: six clearly separated sections with sub-services and conversion paths.
- Reusable category data shared between Home and Services.
- Localize labels and copy in FR/EN, with natural French, not literal word-for-word translation.
- No invented project examples, screenshots or outcome statistics.
