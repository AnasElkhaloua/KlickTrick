# Homepage — Content and Layout

## Goal
Explain Klick Trick's full-service value and move visitors to Contact or WhatsApp. Maintain WEBYTE's visual grammar while replacing inappropriate template sections.

## Section order (approved proposed baseline)
1. Navigation: branded logo, navigation, locale switch, free quote.
2. Hero: French-first premium proposition, concise supporting copy, three-image fanned composition, primary quote CTA -> Contact and secondary service discovery CTA.
3. Services: six category cards in responsive grid; last/adjacent custom-project CTA as appropriate.
4. Why Klick Trick: differentiators grounded in service approach and collaboration, no unverified proof.
5. How We Work: Discovery, Strategy, Execution, Optimization; honest process description, not a guaranteed outcome.
6. Who We Help: startups, e-commerce, professionals, established companies.
7. Final CTA: free quote -> Contact; WhatsApp alternative.
8. Footer.

## Explicit removals/replacements
- Remove WEBYTE brand logo and copy.
- Remove IBM/mParticle marquee until genuine client rights/proof exist.
- Remove fabricated statistics.
- Remove pricing and billing selector entirely.
- Remove template testimonials and web-hosting reviews.
- Do not invent case studies or awards.
- No empty Blog section; Blog and Work will be added when real content exists.

## Visual treatment
Hero and card language should resemble inspected desktop Figma. Adapt section heights to genuine content rather than forcing original 5627px length. Mobile: legible headings, stacked or scrollable cards, uncluttered image composition, functional menu and CTA.
