# Brand & Positioning

## Brand
Klick Trick — premium full-service digital agency serving Morocco.

## Core proposition (proposed copy; editable)
FR: « Une agence. Toutes vos ambitions digitales. »
EN: “One agency. Every digital ambition.”

FR: « Klick Trick accompagne les entreprises marocaines dans leur développement digital grâce à une approche qui réunit stratégie, créativité, technologie et performance. »
EN: “Klick Trick helps Moroccan businesses build, grow and strengthen their digital presence through strategy, creativity, technology and performance.”

## Brand personality
Confident, modern, creative, credible, clear, approachable, business-oriented. Avoid clichés and unsupported promises such as guaranteed rankings or instant revenue.

## Audience
- Startups
- E-commerce companies
- Professionals
- Established businesses

## Trust policy
The agency is newly created and legally established. Do not imply a long agency history. No invented clients, logos, projects, testimonials, awards, metrics or certifications. Do not publish legal registration identifiers until supplied and verified.

## Calls to action
Primary: FR « Demander un devis gratuit » / EN “Request a free quote” -> localized Contact page, ideally `#contact-form`.
Secondary: FR « Nous contacter » / EN “Contact us”.
WhatsApp: floating global button, plus contextual contact option. Number and prefilled messages configured, not hardcoded with fake values.
